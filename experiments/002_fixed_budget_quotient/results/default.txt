Interpretation:

- The observer performs exactly 128 assays at every timestep with fixed radius=3 and horizon=3.
- The operational quotient changes from 5 to 7 to 8 classes under the same assay budget.
- It also temporarily coarsens from 8 back to 7 at t=9, then refines again at t=11.

This is a useful sanity result for the theory: operational identity can be transient and reversible even when the physical update rule and observer budget are fixed.

It is still not evidence of learning. The next experiment must distinguish mere state-dependent context variation from a persistent history-dependent reduction in the cost of exposing a consequential difference.
