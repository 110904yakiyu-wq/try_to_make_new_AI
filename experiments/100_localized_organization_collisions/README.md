# Experiment 100 — localized organization collisions

## Question

Experiment 099 found 130 two-window seeds that generate bounded, persistent localized activity organizations under the unchanged finite-speed mutual-modulation kernel.

Can two such organizations interact and generate a persistent organization that is not reducible to either parent or to any localized organization already reachable from a single two-window seed?

No object boundary, collision handler, reproduction rule, fitness value, organization ID, or interaction-specific rewrite is added.

## Setup

Use the same width-4, 60-window ring and local symmetric kernel as Experiment 099.

First exhaust all 225 non-zero ordered two-window seeds and retain the 130 seeds whose isolated dynamics converge to the localized-active class.

Two organizations are then embedded in the same otherwise-zero ring at controlled start-position separations.

A collision is detected when the combined trajectory first differs from the simple union of the two separately evolved trajectories, or when the separately evolved supports first overlap.

Combined outcomes are classified as:

- independent: no collision within the assay horizon;
- static/quiescent;
- localized active cycle;
- expansive active cycle;
- expansive non-cycle within the finite horizon.

For localized collision products, construct a microcycle key invariant to temporal phase within the cycle and to spatial translation compatible with the three-phase local update schedule.

The isolated two-window-seed catalog contains 126 distinct localized microcycle keys.

A collision product is called `localized_novel_catalog` only when its resulting localized microcycle is absent from that entire isolated catalog, not merely different from the two immediate parents.

## Identical-organization collision result

For both left-rotation and right-rotation kernels the aggregate result is exactly the same.

### Separation 3

All 130 organizations interact immediately.

Outcomes:

- localized active: **102**
- static: **12**
- expansive non-cycle: **16**

All **102 / 102** localized products are absent from the complete isolated-seed catalog.

### Separation 4

All 130 interact.

- localized active: **30**
- expansive cycle: **4**
- expansive non-cycle: **96**

Again, all **30 / 30** localized products are absent from the isolated catalog.

### Separation 5

- independent/no detected collision: **66**
- localized active collision product: **48**
- expansive non-cycle: **16**

All **48 / 48** localized products are absent from the isolated catalog.

At separations 6, 8, and 10, 114/130 organizations remain independent over the collision horizon and the remaining 16 expand rather than settle to a localized product.

## Heterogeneous collision sample

To verify that the result is not peculiar to collisions between identical organizations, use one deterministic heterogeneous partner per localized seed, producing 130 distinct parent-pair trials per separation.

For the left-rotation kernel:

- separation 3: 57 localized products;
- separation 4: 74 localized products;
- separation 5: 40 localized products;
- separation 6: 28 localized products;
- separation 8: 1 localized product.

Every one of these localized products is absent from the 126-cycle isolated-seed catalog.

The right-rotation kernel shows the same qualitative result, with 57, 70, 44, 25, and 2 novel localized products respectively.

## Interpretation boundary

This is the first experiment in the project where naturally generated metastable organizations interact and frequently settle into a third persistent organization without an externally supplied object grammar.

The narrow claim is:

> localized activity organizations generated by the same minimal local law can interact nonlinearly, and collision can create a new bounded persistent microcycle that is not reachable from any isolated two-window seed in the tested catalog.

This is stronger than mere trajectory interference.

However, it is still not reproduction, heredity, open-ended evolution, or intelligence.

The `novel` label is relative to the exhaustive **two-window isolated-seed** catalog. A collision product could still be reachable from a larger isolated initial condition. Therefore the result should not be described as creation of an absolutely unprecedented state or organization.

The heterogeneous assay is also a deterministic 130-pair sample rather than an exhaustive all-pairs sweep.

## Next question

Does collision-generated organization itself behave as an organization rather than as a one-off dynamical residue?

The next audit should take the new localized products and test:

1. one-bit internal and boundary perturbations;
2. collision with one of their parent organizations;
3. whether products can themselves generate further localized products;
4. whether product robustness or interaction behavior differs systematically from first-generation organizations.

If collision products remain metastable and participate in further organization-changing collisions, the substrate begins to support a genuine endogenous ecology of organizations rather than a fixed catalog of isolated local cycles.
