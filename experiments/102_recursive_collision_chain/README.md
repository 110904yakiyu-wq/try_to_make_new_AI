# Experiment 102 — recursive collision chain

> **Correction from Experiment 103:** this experiment inherited Experiment 099's convenience label `localized = cycle support <= 13 windows`. Later ring-size scaling showed that the cycles labelled `expansive_cycle` below are actually spatially bounded: their period and finite support remain unchanged on rings of 60, 90, and 120 windows. Therefore the raw labels in this experiment are retained as historical classifier output, but the earlier claim that the bounded chain necessarily terminates is withdrawn. See Experiment 103.

## Question

Experiments 100–101 established that localized organizations can collide to form new localized products and that those products themselves retain metastable organization-class persistence.

Can a collision-generated organization participate in another organization-changing collision, or is novelty limited to one generation?

## Generational notation

This experiment uses observer-side labels only for bookkeeping:

- `G0`: localized microcycles generated by isolated two-window seeds;
- `G1`: localized products from the separation-3 collision of two identical G0 organizations;
- `G2`: localized products obtained by colliding a G1 organization with its original G0 parent;
- `G3`: products obtained by another collision with that same G0 parent.

No generation label exists in the substrate.

A product is counted as novel when its canonical cycle key is absent from the earlier tested catalogs.

## Setup

Use the width-4 finite-speed local kernel and the 60-window quiescent ring.

For every collision stage choose a phase-0 frame of the existing organization with maximal active support. The added G0 parent is positioned at a phase-compatible location (translation by a multiple of three windows) so its isolated dynamics remain equivalent to the original parent.

Cases with initial support overlap are excluded rather than called collisions.

For accepted cases, interaction is verified causally by comparing the joint trajectory against the union of the separately evolved trajectories. A case is only advanced when those trajectories actually diverge or their independently evolved supports meet.

## Raw Experiment-102 result

The aggregate is exactly the same for left-rotation and right-rotation kernels.

### G0 -> G1

From Experiment 100:

- isolated G0 catalog: **126 distinct bounded microcycles** from 130 localized seeds;
- novel G1 products: **102 distinct microcycles**.

### G1 + G0 parent -> G2

At the clean adjacent placement used here:

- 32/102 candidate G1 states overlap the added parent at initialization and are excluded;
- remaining clean cases: **70**;
- interacting cases classified localized by the old threshold: **70 / 70**;
- novel G2 microcycles outside G0 and G1 catalogs: **70 / 70**;
- distinct G2 keys: **70**.

### G2 + same G0 parent

All 70 cases interact.

The old classifier reported:

- `localized`: **38 / 70**;
- `expansive_cycle`: **32 / 70**.

Experiment 103 showed that this split is not a real bounded-vs-expanding split. Instead:

- 38 cycles have period 6 and support 13;
- 32 cycles have period 12 and support 14;
- all 70 remain exactly the same finite support/period on rings 60, 90, and 120.

So all 70 are bounded under the stronger scaling criterion.

### Next collision

The 38 period-6/support-13 cases were collided again.

The old classifier labelled all 38 resulting cycles `expansive_cycle` because their support exceeded 13.

Experiment 103 showed that these are actually:

- period **6**;
- support **16**;
- ring-size invariant across 60/90/120.

Thus the statement in the original version of this file that the chain had saturated at this point was incorrect.

## Corrected interpretation

The positive result remains valid and is stronger than originally stated:

> collision-generated bounded organizations can themselves act as causal participants in later collisions, producing further bounded persistent cycles outside earlier tested catalogs.

However, Experiment 103 also shows that continued collision growth becomes structurally repetitive: support can keep increasing while low-order local motif repertoires saturate.

Therefore the corrected conclusion is **bounded accretion without demonstrated open-ended organizational novelty**.

## Why this remains useful

No period label, species label, reproduction operator, mutation operator, object boundary, or collision table is supplied.

The only hard substrate is the local mutual-modulation law plus the three-phase finite-speed update geometry. `G0/G1/G2/...` are retrospective observer labels over naturally arising bounded cycles.

## Next boundary

Longer externally fed accretion is no longer the interesting target.

The next stronger question is whether one bounded organization can create an additional spatially separated bounded organization from generic material or perturbation without the experimenter inserting another valid parent seed.

That is the boundary toward endogenous fission/reproduction rather than externally supplied growth.
