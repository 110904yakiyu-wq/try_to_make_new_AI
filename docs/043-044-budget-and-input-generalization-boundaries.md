# docs/043–044 — Matching-budget and new-cohort input sensitivity

**Experiment 143.** Using the exact 1000-candidate on-cycle matching proposals and Experiment140's first-eligible control per parent, evaluate **nested** matched cohorts for B=1,2,5,10,25,50,75,100,150,200,300,400,500,750,1000. The strict ever-10-position spread contrast remains negative at every budget, from −16.97 pp at 100/55 parents to −12.95 pp at 1000/113 parents, although cohort composition changes. Bounds for ever-60 are also strictly negative. The original 137 1–100-budget results match exactly. These points are neither independent replications nor evidence of a population-wide causal treatment effect.

**Experiment 144.** On the 58 newly covered parents only, both injection sides and all raw 4-bit material values from 1 through 15 show negative G0–first-control cumulative spread contrast at the two preexisting 10/60-position thresholds. Residual right censoring at the 60-window threshold is counted as uncertainty. These are repeated probes from the same selected parents, not independent tests of a cognition mechanism.

**Updated inferential boundary.** Extended pre-response matching demonstrates that the *first-choice* negative spread association is not an artifact restricted to the oldest 55-pair matched sample. However, Experiment142 shows sign reversal is possible under outcome-adversarial **selection from the expanded 970 finite eligible controls**. Accordingly, do not claim that all valid counterfactual cycles are more expansive than G0 or that bit-level/temporal organizations are an identified causal mediator. No new physical or cognitive primitive has been added.
