# 035 — Direction and raw-material robustness boundary (Experiment 135)

The frozen Experiment 130 response population contains 55 matched G0/control parents and 30 paired interventions per parent (3,300 arm-level records). Experiment 135 is a **read-only reanalysis** by left/right boundary and raw value 1–15. It does not add an independent experimental population or modify physical dynamics.

At spread threshold 10, confirmed G0−control rate differences are −19.64 percentage points for left-side insertion and −14.30 points for right-side insertion. For threshold 60 they are [−18.30,−18.18] and −12.36 points, respectively. Both thresholds have a strictly negative upper bound for all 15 raw values separately. Censoring remains unknown unless an exact state+phase recurrence certifies noncrossing.

Inference: this is robustness to predefined input-stratum partitioning *within the selected 55 parents*. It is not new causal evidence for specific higher-level organization. The 55/130 matching eligibility problem persists.
