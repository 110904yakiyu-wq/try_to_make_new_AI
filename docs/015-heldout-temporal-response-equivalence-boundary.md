# 015 — Held-out early-response equivalence boundary (Experiment 115)

**Audited through Experiment 115.** The finite-speed 104 kernel and the frozen 112 twenty-parent exact 108 cycle-union/phase/first-tick-mask cohort are unchanged. All 113 strongest-tier physical-feature alternative bit assignments were enumerated, excluding the true donor patch identity. Experiment 115 splits material probes into six training inputs (`1,7,15` at both boundaries) and 24 held-out inputs (the other 12 raw values, both boundaries). The intervention selection never consults those held-out response measurements.

## Findings

1. There are **23/14/20 feature-qualified donor patches** of widths 1/2/3, containing **33/59/414** distinct physical-null alternative microstates. These inventories reproduce Experiment 113.
2. Exact equality to G0-*transplant* causal response-position masks at training tick 1 retains **12/9/20 patches** with **16/32/392 alternatives**. Requiring masks also at training tick 3 retains **zero** alternatives for *all* widths. Hence this tighter estimand is **not identifiable within the enumerated null support**.
3. Held-out tick-24 G0-parent response-mask Jaccard contrasts, equal-parent and within-tier-eligible-patch weighted (donor minus mean null), are: width 1: **+0.02007** under full physics, **-0.03321** with tick-1 gating; width 2: **+0.07849**, **-0.00722**; width 3: **+0.15671**, **+0.15845**. Width-3 parent signs are **9 higher, 7 lower**, so the pooled-looking positive gap is not evidence of a robust universal transfer advantage.
4. Held-out reference class disagreements at 500 ticks form an extremely limited retrospective 8-probe/6-parent pool, and class outcomes of candidate interventions may be censored. Treat the class comparison as exploratory, not a prospective validation set. Finite tick-6/12/24 response masks are always directly observable and do not rely on class convergence.

## Hard inference limits

- **No unique higher-level organizational cause identified.** Raw values, unmeasured detailed microstate arrangements, and subsequent nonlinear updates differ under all these conditions. Even exact early *position masks* do not imply equal bitwise effects or equal physical histories.
- A null set empty at strict training equivalence is a **positivity failure**, not proof of the desired special cause and not evidence of a zero causal effect.
- The fixed six/24 input split is a *protocol holdout* within a cohort whose outcomes were explored in Experiments 104–114. It is not new independent unseen data. Using prior resolved class-disagreement keys further weakens independence; present those separately.
- 57 donor patches, their 506 alternatives and 480 held-out parent/probe pairs do **not** provide that many independent samples; inference must be parent-pair clustered, with conditioning eligibility disclosed.
- The 500-tick `censored` category does not mean dead, no persistence, no fission or nonreproduction; no new fission or heredity assay has been run.
- Nothing in this audit establishes a learned objective, interpretation, meaning, SELF, autonomous replication, evolutionary innovation, or an irreducible history primitive.

## Decision for Experiment 116

Retain exactly the same physical kernel and 112 parents. Replace the all-or-nothing 6-probe tick-1+tick-3 matching with an **outcome-blind, graded distance** over early response masks, preferably prespecified and stratified by the physical-null pool and by parent. Measure the held-out tick-24 geometry contrast as a diagnostic only, and report how common support changes with a fixed approximation tolerance. Never select distance thresholds or controls using held-out future responses.
