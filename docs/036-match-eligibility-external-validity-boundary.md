# 036 — Matched-cohort coverage and extrapolation boundary (Experiment 136)

Frozen Experiment 133: 130 G0 parents; 55 (42.31%) have at least one qualifying on-cycle, exact-geometry control in the first 100 autonomous proposals, and 75 do not. Period 6: 26/40 eligible; period 12: 29/76; other periods: 0/14. Seed popcount 6 or 7: 0/16 eligible. These eligibility dimensions are interdependent; stratified counts are descriptive, not fitted causal effects.

The confirmed negative spread-rate contrast from Experiment 130 is **conditional on the 55 eligible parents**. For the other 75, the 100-attempt protocol does not supply a counterfactual control. Even if one imagines a hypothetical valid contrast for all 75 and allows its binary outcomes to range freely, the arithmetic 130-parent G0−control envelope is:

- Threshold 10: [−2530,+1970]/3900 = [−64.87%,+50.51%].
- Threshold 60: [−2503,+1998]/3900 = [−64.18%,+51.23%].

These are **hypothetical completion bounds, not identified all-parent causal effects**. Failure to find a control within 100 proposals is not proof of nonexistence outside that search budget. No positive/negative event is assigned to unmatched parents.
