# 009 — Organizational persistence is not reproduction

## Experiment chain

- 099: 130 phase-sensitive G0 localized active seeds. Coarse organizational persistence after one-bit damage.
- 100–103: collisions can generate new bounded active organizations, but repeated externally supplied accretion is not open-ended novelty.
- 104: 36/3900 strict material-triggered fragmentations into 72 independently viable localized daughters. The historical counts require phase-aligned CENTER=30; CENTER=20 is a different physical assay.
- 105: strict fission is similarly rare for support-randomized compact frames, while whole outcome distributions differ strongly because most random controls have not converged to an active cycle.
- 106: placing random controls on *their own* autonomously reached phase-0 cycles reduces that disparity, but unmatched period/support confounds remain.
- 107: requiring equal period, equal cycle-union support size and equal phase-0 occupied positions leaves 54 matched parents (out of 130). Across 1620 interventions per group, strict fission is 2 vs 3, while connected-active outcomes are G0 1455 vs control 1192 and censored outcomes are 143 vs 390.

## Claim hierarchy

1. **Established in this finite deterministic toy:** raw local interactions can create persistent bounded activity, collision-created organizations and occasional two-daughter fragmentation.
2. **Supported with matched observational controls:** particular naturally formed on-cycle G0 microorganizations persist as connected active cycles more often than selected alternative compact cycles under single-window material perturbations.
3. **Not established:** reproduction, heredity, self-generated goal, intrinsic fitness, meta-learning, environment-specific adaptation or organization-driven enhancement of daughter production.
4. **Unresolved:** causal role of temporal relational structure versus remaining motif/distribution differences, as well as right-censoring in long nonconvergent trajectories.

## No-go against convenient labels

Observer-side period, support, daughter count and G-generation are measurement devices, not physical fields or internal labels. A difference in outcome histograms alone cannot be promoted to an endogenous purpose to persist.

The 107 subset is strongly conditioned by a finite random search: only 54/130 parents pass the exact phase-0 occupancy filter after at most 100 candidates. The result is an audited conditional statement, not a population claim.

## Next falsification gate

Before adding a new kernel primitive:

- Match complete cycle-union support **positions**, not merely its size or one phase's occupancy. Disclose acceptance/coverage.
- Measure persistence with fixed horizons and censored-aware trajectories; do not call timeout a loss.
- Test causal replacement of a *temporal* context (phase-consistent repeated local orbit segment), preserving instantaneous microstate where possible. In a deterministic Markov kernel, identical full microstate and phase imply identical future, so only coarse, budget-bounded organization claims are meaningful.
- Test multiple raw perturbation positions and rotations to expose scheduler-specific response.

If stronger matching erases the connected-activity difference, classify Experiment 107 as a residual geometry effect rather than an organization-specific persistence effect.

## Implementation policy

No learning objective, organism ID, boundary, reward, reproduction instruction, memory register or self variable is added for these observer assays. New kernel mechanisms require failure of the existing substrate under a specified counterfactual test.
