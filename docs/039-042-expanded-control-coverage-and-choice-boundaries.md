# docs/039–042 — Extended autonomous matching and comparator-choice limits

Experiments 139–142 reuse the finite-speed three-phase 60-window local mutual-modulation kernel of Experiments 104/108 and **do not add cognition, reward, memory, agents or new physical assumptions**.

**Exp139: matching coverage.** Extend 100 autonomous control attempts per G0 to 1000 using the identical seeded CPython candidate generator and a 500-tick autonomous period/support/phase0-occupancy eligibility test. The original 55 parents and 87 accepted candidates are exactly reproduced at 100 attempts. With 1000 attempts, 113/130 parents have eligible controls, with 970 total accepted control states. Newly covered = 58 parents; residual 17 remain without a detected control, not proven structurally impossible.

**Exp140: new-parent spread.** Freeze each parent's *first* accepted control before any raw material assay. The new 58 parents have G0-control ever-10-window spread difference −9.14 pp, and ever-60-window difference bounded between −8.22 and −8.16 pp (50,000-tick observation, complete recurrence certificates for non-arrivals). All 113 parents combined give −12.95 pp (10) and [−11.68,−11.62] pp (60). All 3300 historical first-100 probe endpoints exactly reproduce Experiment 130; 64 newly covered probes agree with independent Python implementation at ≤500 ticks.

**Exp141: eligibility-gate audit.** Across 130,000 proposals, right-censored by autonomous tick500: 89,999; static 1,185; wrong period 28,226; wrong cycle-union support 9,580; wrong phase0 occupancy 40; accepted 970. The counts are *first failing gate* categories. Candidate right censoring may obscure viable alternatives, particularly for residual unmatched parents. Zero success in 1000 tries is no nonexistence proof.

**Exp142: comparator-choice non-identification.** Evaluate all 970 eligible controls × 30 raw probes (29,100 alternatives). Historical 100-budget 134 envelope is reproduced exactly. On 113 parents, first-choice G0–control spread difference is negative; uniform-per-parent eligible-control averaging is also negative. **But** arbitrary one-eligible-control-per-parent choice yields 10-position contrast interval [−40.09,+6.64] pp and 60-position interval [−33.42,+5.10] pp. The envelope spans zero; earlier sign robustness was only relative to the first 100-proposal finite candidate set, not all detected eligible controls. Endpoints are retrospective sensitivity extrema and cannot be used as a fair control-choice protocol.

**Conclusions.** A large fraction of the previous 75 unmatched parents suffered *finite candidate budget* limitations. Stronger coverage remains associated with lower G0 spread under two precommitted comparator selectors, but that association is not invariant to all admissible physical controls and has no identified organization-specific causal origin. Parent clustering, remaining 17 unmatchable-within-budget cases, bit-level confounding and autonomous candidate censoring remain binding inferential constraints. Never promote these findings to learning, autonomous goals, reproduction, SELF or intelligence.
