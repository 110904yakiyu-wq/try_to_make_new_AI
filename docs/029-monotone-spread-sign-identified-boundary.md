# 029 — Monotone full-ring spread can be sign-identified without solving recurrent-cycle viability (Exp129)

**Question.** The frozen 55 matched G0/control parent pairs from Experiments 108/125–127 yield a wider-than-zero partial-identification interval for **eventual recurrent connected class** after 50,000 ticks. Experiment 129 holds physical laws, starting states and all 30 material probes constant, but changes the target to a distinct physical-history event: *has any nonzero raw window appeared at every physical position in the 60-window ring at any time?*

**Sound certification.** Positive = the nonzero historical union reaches all 60 positions. Negative = a complete physical microstate+next-scheduler-phase recurrence has been observed before the union reaches all positions. Negative then holds indefinitely because the whole future cycle has been traversed and must repeat. All other cases remain right censored. This does not require labeling an unresolved recurrent class as failed or viable.

**Frozen-cohort findings.** At 500 ticks, G0 has 129 positive, 1,507 negative certified and 14 unresolved; control has 369 positive, 1,251 negative and 30 unresolved. The G0-minus-control **ever-full-rate** belongs to **[−16.36,−13.70] percentage points** already at 500 ticks. At 50,000, G0=143 positive, 1,507 negative, 0 unresolved; control=395 positive, 1,254 negative, 1 unresolved. Its rate difference is **[−253/1650,−252/1650] = [−15.33,−15.27] percentage points**. Among parents: 10 definitely higher spread in G0, 37 definitely higher in controls, eight indeterminate or tied. The sign of this distinct estimand is identified; the recurrent connected-class sign remains unidentified under Exp127.

**Interpretation and limitation.** This is evidence of **differential propensity for physical global propagation** under the same fixed kernel, *conditional* on preselected exact-cycle support-matched parents (55/130). The observational endpoint was proposed after inspecting Exp128 censor patterns and is therefore exploratory, not a pre-registered confirmation. Parent and probe dependence, raw bit-content confounding, selection, finite resource constraints, and ring-size assumptions remain. Full historical spread is not permanently expansive occupancy, organism death, failed reproduction, learning, SELF, or an autonomous goal. There is no new primitive or added recognizer influencing physical execution.

**Next prioritized audit:** prespecify threshold family (e.g., first visit to at least 10,20,30,40,50,60 positions) and check that directional conclusions are not an artifact of the 60/60 cutoff. Keep the same frozen cohorts and correct event-time negative certificates.
