# 030 — G0/control physical-spread difference survives threshold sweep (Experiment130)

All of Experiments 125–130 use the unchanged 60-window phase-aware 4-bit finite-speed physical kernel and the same 55 exact-on-cycle parent/control pairs, 30 material injection conditions per arm per pair. Experiment129 changes the endpoint *explicitly* from eventual connected recurrent class to the **monotone historical event** of ever visiting every physical ring window with nonzero contents. Experiment130 checks a family of such events with thresholds **K = 10,20,30,40,50,60** distinct positions reached.

A yes certificate is the first threshold hit. A no certificate is a full microstate+next-phase recurrence with the corresponding threshold not hit throughout the whole preperiod and one cycle. All other cases remain right censored. Therefore the eventual G0−control threshold-hit rate has sharp conservative bounds. For each K, the 50,000-tick bounds exclude zero **on the negative side**: K10 −16.97pp, K20 [−15.39,−15.33]pp, K30 [−15.27,−15.21]pp, K40/K50/K60 [−15.33,−15.27]pp. Their finite-time 500-tick bounds also exclude zero. Every one of the 3,300 first-hit times for K60 independently matches the previously committed Experiment129 data.

This reduces the concern that the direction exists only because the maximal full-ring threshold was selected after examining Experiment128. It is not an independent replication or evidence for a mechanism: all K values are nested functions of the same deterministic material-probe orbits; raw initial bit values differ; 75 unmatched parents are excluded; and no target causality was isolated. Relative resistance to **physical propagation** must not be promoted to endogenous purpose, SELF, reproduction, learning, memory, or unconditionally superior eventual connected-cycle persistence. The latter remains nonidentified by Experiment127.
