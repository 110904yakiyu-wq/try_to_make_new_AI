## Causal discrimination tested

The earlier 112 response-transfer advantage compared a G0 internal bit patch against sparse random assignments preserving only the recipient's first unperturbed tick changed-position mask. To audit simpler physical explanations, Experiment 113 enumerates **all distinct**, nonzero, recipient-different raw-bit assignments at the edited patch positions and removes exact G0 patch identity. The candidates must preserve the common first-tick changed-position set. Further exact conditions match the donor-patched microstate's total raw popcount, circular adjacent bitwise AND popcount, and total first-tick bit flips in progressively stricter strata.

Null eligibility is determined using **unperturbed physics only**. The frozen 112 30 material probes and the original 112 G0/control reference *resolved class-disagreement* keys define downstream response scoring. All finite alternatives are scored, so null Monte Carlo variance is absent. The estimand is uniform over the finite eligible alternative assignments, not a general population causal effect. Parent pairs are aggregation units, not null microstates, patches, or probe conditions.

## Observations

- Exactly the same **20/55/130** first-mask/equal-cycle/G0 parent pipeline was reproduced. The reference had only **eight** class-informative parents, with ten resolved class-discordant probes in total.
- G0 donor patches preserving first-mask numbered **35 / 16 / 20** for widths 1/2/3. At least one distinct alternative also preserving first-mask existed for **34 / 16 / 20**; after popcount and AND matches for **23 / 14 / 20** at the AND tier. The first-tick flip match did not reduce these patch counts further, but narrowed some pools.
- Width-1 parent mean donor minus exhaustive mask-only-null **+0.17500** (8 informative parents); with popcount matched null **−0.04048** (7 informative parents); with popcount+AND(+flips) matched null **+0.10000** (5 informative parents). Width-2 and width-3 final-tier differences were **+0.10000** (4 parents) and **+0.02451** (6 parents).
- Crucial selection-adjusted descriptive contrast on the **same popcount-eligible width-1 patches**: donor-minus-mask-null **+0.05714** vs donor-minus-popcount-null **−0.04048**. Different tier eligibility changes the parent and patch population; comparisons without this restriction confound sample selection with null matching.
- Explicit censoring: width-1 mask tier has **67 censored / 113** alternative probe applications on informative patches; width-1 popcount tier has **12 / 29**. Those applications are correlated and are not independent parent-level trials. A censored outcome is unresolved at 500 ticks and counted as **unconfirmed**, not dead or inactive.

## Interpretation

The 112 apparent positive raw-bit transfer association is **not robust under one basic physical feature constraint**. That rules out a simple assertion of a demonstrated G0-specific local causal code on these observations. It **does not** identify popcount as the mediator; the mask-only-to-popcount change includes a different conditional null distribution, and feature restrictions can induce selection. More restrictive matched tiers exhibit both positive and weak null effects on tiny subsets. The still-uncontrolled spatial microstate, second and subsequent tick transitions, attractor entry, and convergence time may fully account for measured variation.

The experiment does not establish organizational agency, learning, repair goal, replication, selfhood, history-only state changes at equal physical states, or autonomy. Markov state and scheduler phase determine a unique future under the fixed kernel.
