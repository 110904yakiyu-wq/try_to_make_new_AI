# 031 — Feature-conditioning of physical spread is selection-sensitive

**Experiment 131.** Frozen Experiment 130 matched cohort of 55 parent pairs and 1,650 paired material probes, unchanged kernel. Physical post-injection initial values and one autonomous update are audited without reselecting any G0/control parents from outcomes. Exact equality filters on popcount, ring-neighbor AND, first autonomous bit flips and exact changed-position set retain respectively 450/15 parents, 168/14, 126/10 and 102/6 paired probes/parents, from the initial 1,650/55.

The `ever >=10 cumulative nonzero sites` G0−control contrast is −16.97 percentage points on all probes, −13.78 pp under popcount matching, +2.38 pp under AND matching, +2.38 pp under one-step flip matching and +12.75 pp under exact first-tick changed-mask matching. The stricter `>=60` contrast also reverses to positive among eligible probes. Importantly, at the `pop_and_flips` stage the pooled >=10 contrast is +2.38 pp, but the **parent-equal** contrast is −4.90 pp. This is a severe dependence on the observational estimand.

Matching post-injection early physical features selects *which* raw-value/side probes are retained. No downstream outcomes were used to select the probes, but the selection is still conditional on physical information causally linked to downstream propagation. The sign reversal must not be interpreted as causal mediation, an emergent temporal grammar, or true recovery/survival behavior. Experiment 132 partitions the full and retained probe populations to identify selection-composition shifts explicitly. The original full-cohort negative physical-spread association remains correct for its own chosen 55-parent estimand. Neither contrast proves organizational autonomy.
