# 045–047 — Raw and phase-aligned autonomous proximity versus spread response

**Frozen 139/140/142 population:** 113/130 G0 parents with at least one of 970 eligible, autonomous, on-cycle raw-physical controls in the first 1000 independent candidate attempts per parent. Same period, cycle-union support positions, instantaneous phase0 occupied support, scheduler phase; same 30 postselection boundary raw material conditions. Physical kernel: 60 window x 4 bits, 3-phase update, CENTER30, bit rotate left; no cognitive primitives.

## Exp145 — Selection by physical proximity before input perturbation

First-accepted controls show G0 minus control **ever-10-window** event rate −12.95 pp and censor-aware **ever-60-window** [−11.68,−11.62] pp at horizon 50,000 / earlier proven recurrence. Choosing nearest raw-Hamming controls (tie by number of differing 4-bit windows, then proposal index) instead gives −8.53 and −6.96 pp. Minimum total-popcount discrepancy gives −8.35 and about −7.25 pp. These selection differences are real, but all studied predeclared rules remain negative. Does not identify a higher-level cause.

## Exp146 — One exact G0 identity and insufficient scalar distance

Across the 970 eligible controls, one state is exactly G0's same physical phase0 microstate (parent22, attempt15), and the others lie on physically distinct autonomous cycles. A physical-null convention requiring nonidentical states would exclude this one. Grouping alternative controls at equal raw-bit Hamming distance within parent yields **231 groups with multiplicity**, of which **198** show different ever10 outcome counts and **199** different ever60 counts. Distances alone cannot predict the response. Raw-distance tie-breaking by proposal attempt versus by number of differing windows changes the nearest choice in 6/113 parents, altering summed control ever10 counts **663 vs 615**. Thus tie-breaking must be part of the estimand.

## Exp147 — Preoutcome autonomous time-slice matching

Select controls using no-injection microstate distances at the same phase-aligned ticks. Rule [0,3,6] gives G0-control ever10 −7.67 pp; ever60 −6.55 pp, versus tick0-only −8.53/−6.96 pp. Tick sets [0,3], [0,1,3,6,12] and [3,6,12] also show negative contrasts. Held-out material responses are exactly frozen Experiment140/142 endpoints, not retuned after selection. Uncertainty of spread60 is retained.

## Strict inference limit

These are exploratory control-selection sensitivity studies on 113 selected parents, not independent evidence of adaptation. A fully identical current physical state and scheduler phase necessarily gives identical continuation. Equal raw bitcount or matching a few early autonomous snapshots do **not** establish identical microstates or pathwise dynamical equivalence. Positive findings of a negative diffusion association do not identify self-preservation, intelligence, learning, autonomous goals, special history variables, or a novel biological reproduction process. The Experiment142 arbitrary eligible-control-choice bound crosses zero. Do not overwrite that caution with favorable choices of a preoutcome rule. No physical kernel modifications.
