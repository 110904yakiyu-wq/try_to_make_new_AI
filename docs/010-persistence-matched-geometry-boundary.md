# 010 — Persistence audit after exact support matching

## Status: audited through Experiment 110

The CPU-AI hobby substrate uses fixed finite-speed local mutual
modulation with no cognitive variable, reward, learner, node, symbolic
program, organism ID or reproduction instruction.

### What remains reproducible

- Experiment 104 phase-aligned raw material injection: 36/3900
  strict two-daughter fragmentations (nonzero-window parents = 130).
  The comparison is scheduler-phase sensitive: G0 seed center must
  satisfy center modulo 3 = 0.
- Experiment 105 full five-category outcome distribution:
  support-matched raw random microstates often have long nonconvergent
  transient trajectories, so raw fission-frequency comparisons are
  severely censored.
- Experiment 106: existing on-cycle random-control trajectories help
  with the convergence confound, but periods and support sizes differ.
- Experiment 107: requiring equal period, union-support SIZE and
  instantaneous occupied sites leaves a 54-parent subset. G0 still
  has more connected active outcomes.
- Experiment 108: requiring exact union-support POSITIONS as well,
  among 55/130 accepted parents (at most 100 random candidates per
  parent), gives 1486 vs 1215 connected active cycles (out of 1650
  one-window material probes each). Strict fission is 2 vs 2.
- Experiment 109: in a six-probe causal transplant assay, the mean
  number of internal 4-bit windows needed to reproduce the donor's
  Boolean response signature is 2.53 for the real G0 donor and 2.57
  for a synthetic donor with changes at identical sites. No
  specific compositional advantage is established.
- Experiment 110: among pairs whose first-tick changed-position
  mask is identical, G0 has a connected-active advantage in 9,
  control in 7, ties in 4. The conditioned subset is very small
  and does not establish mediation.

### Prohibited inference

Do NOT translate the connected-active difference into reproduction,
learning, survival goals, autopoiesis or creation of a SELF. In the
strictly deterministic Markov kernel, full current microstate+phase
determines the entire continuation. The observer may choose coarse
quotients, but history-only changes at equal full states are
impossible by construction.

The remaining unmeasured quantities include detailed local masks,
phase-consistent temporal response and physical work needed to recover
from perturbations. All controls are selected *before* testing raw
material outcomes, but rare eligibility (55/130) and unequal
nonconvergence remain important limitations.

### Next prioritized experiment

Conditional causal intervention: within an exact union-support and
phase-aligned parent pair, replace one or more local raw windows while
retaining the same immediate changed-position mask. Compare the
follow-on organization-class response against matched-mask random
replacements, not unconditioned compact random patterns. Use a fixed
probe budget and parent-level sampling. If no excess response transfer
remains, favor a low-level bit/mask explanation over metastable
organizational autonomy.

Keep negative results and correction commits. Do not add a new
learning, regeneration or replication rule merely to make a
positive outcome appear.
