# 037 — Finite control-search budget sensitivity (Experiment 137)

Using only Experiment 133's frozen accepted-attempt lists, apply search budgets 1, 2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 75 and 100. Keep the **first** qualifying control under each budget, so its future response is the historically archived Exp130 first-choice response. Do not inspect intervention outcomes during selection.

Matching rates (parents out of 130) by budget: 1, 2, 5, 10, 19, 35, 48 and 55, respectively. Negative G0−control spread differences hold at thresholds 10 and 60 in every budget-specific eligible subset. Values at small budgets rely on only 1–5 parents and must not be treated as statistical verification. This analysis does not simulate or establish coverage for budgets over 100.

Changes to observed magnitude arise with changes to the selected parent population; they do not identify a causal matching-budget effect.
