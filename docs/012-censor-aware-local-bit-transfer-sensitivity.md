# 012 — Conditional raw-bit transfer boundary (through Experiment 111)

## Status: audited through Experiment 111

Experiments 108–110 established a conditional association between a naturally
reachable G0 local active cycle and its connected-active outcomes after
bounded raw-material injections. Exact autonomous cycle geometry, period,
phase-0 occupancy and first-tick changed-site conditioning do not establish
an autonomous *cause* at an organizational level.

The **primary** Experiment 111 tests contiguous patch widths 1, 2 and 3 using only *resolved,
discordant* donor/recipient material probes. Although 20 parents have matched
first-tick physical masks, only **two** parents have informative resolved
reference probes under six fixed material tests. In that two-parent subset,
confirmed donor-minus-null fractions were **0.000** for width 1, **+0.500**
for width 2 and **-0.125** for width 3. No direction is supported as a
reliable composition effect. That primary result is archived in
`experiments/111_conditional_local_bit_swap/README.md`.

A separate **secondary response-distance sensitivity audit** makes a
narrowly defined physical intervention. For each of
the 20 initial changed-mask-matched G0/control pairs (among 55 exact-geometry
matches of 130 parents), it inserts one G0 raw 4-bit window value into its
matched on-cycle control. It requires the same unperturbed first-tick changed
positions and compares the intervention to same-site nonzero replacement
values satisfying that mask. A stricter null also equates the 4-bit Hamming
distance from recipient content. Null sampling uses deterministic seeds,
never future-response outcomes. Physical law and scheduler are frozen.

The *secondary* six-probe 500-tick outcome is categorical:
`connected_active` / `other_cycle` / `censored`.
`censored` must never be treated as a failure or a nonviable state.

Across 34 eligible sites in 20 parents, 27 sites in 16 parents had distinct
original donor/recipient *categorical* signatures. Unlike the primary assay,
this Hamming distance includes comparisons where one reference was censored:
thus **16 does not replace the primary two-parent informative denominator**.
 Mean G0-directed gain toward the donor
signature was **0.000 per parent**, against **-0.448** for the same-mask null
and **-0.781** for the tighter same-bitflip null. Only 2/27 informative local
replacements reproduced the full six-probe G0 signature. The positive
G0-minus-null excess is predominantly random-replacement deterioration, not
an average absolute transfer advantage.

When also conditioning on the first-tick changed positions for **every one
of the six material-injected states** (including null replacements), eligible
sites fell to 10 across 7 parents (informative 9 across 6 parents). Mean
actual gain was +0.333 in the same-mask comparison and +0.167 in the
same-bitflip comparable subset; this tiny selected stratum is insufficient to
identify any distinct temporal causal mechanism.

The censor-aware metric is a sensitivity analysis of finite-observation
classes, not a demonstration of *confirmed* G0 response transfer. The
primary Experiment 111 result, with censoring excluded from the reference
identification denominator, is more conservative for mechanistic claims.

The crucial asymmetry remains: G0 donor and original recipient are pretested
on-cycle; one-window hybrids and same-site random alternatives need not be.
This assay is therefore a test of local response transfer and configuration
compatibility, not a controlled exchange of two complete dynamical
organizations. There is no evidence of reproduction, learning, SELF, survival
goals, or an intrinsic history variable. In a deterministic Markov kernel,
current full microstate plus scheduler phase fixes continuation.

### Contract for the next study

- Treat parent as analysis unit and retain finite-sample selection fractions.
- Fix candidate interventions and controls before material-response outcomes.
- Preserve instantaneous physical execution masks as appropriate, including
  material-conditioned masks if seeking stricter mediation control.
- Avoid picking a best-performing patch on the same probes used for evaluation.
- Prefer phase-consistent multi-window transfer with independent probe
  validation, plus censoring/horizon sensitivity, before proposing a new
  organizing principle or changing the kernel.
- Preserve negative results and reproducible code rather than retrofitting
  positive interpretation to the substrate.
