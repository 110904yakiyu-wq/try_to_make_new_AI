# 020–022 — Source-proximity repair with equal edit budget; remaining positivity and censor boundaries

**Audited Experiments 120, 121 and 122.** The earlier G0 patch transplantation assays had a deterministic source-bit proximity advantage compared with all distinct same-site bit alternatives (Exp118 theorem). Exp119 equalized that distance by an additional edit, changing edit budget. Exp120 therefore exchanges one edited site for another while preserving **exactly k changed occupied windows** (k=1/2/3), equal raw 4-bit Hamming distance to the original G0 target, first autonomous changed-position mask, global popcount, neighboring bit AND sum, and total first-tick flips. No injected response is inspected for null eligibility.

Of 23/14/20 contexts qualified in Exp113 strongest physical tier, only 3/5/7 permit any alternative obeying **all** features and equal edit count, among 910/11,760/181,104 enumerated local edit assignments. The available candidate states total only 3/10/15 across contexts; source G0 and recipient remain physically distinct, so matching on a few scalar features never determines the full trajectory.

**Exp121 held-out response-mask similarity:** with the same 6 train-probe selection (raw 1,7,15, t1/t3) and 24 test probes (other 12 values on 2 sides, t6/t12/t24), donor minus equal-budget null tick-24 Jaccard is −0.04530/+0.04481/+0.08090 over 2/4/5 parent clusters. For width 3, only 1 parent has a positive donor difference, 2 negative, 2 tied. The comparator still swaps *which* sites are edited, so specific spatial interactions remain uncontrolled. Nothing here identifies a higher-level causal mechanism.

**Exp122 orbit outcomes:** for identical eligibility and pretrained selected nulls, connected active cycle identified by 500 ticks: widths 1/2/3 G0 transplant=83.33/87.50/53.75%; equal-budget null=89.58/93.75/42.92%. Yet censor rates are donor=16.67/10.42/44.17%, null=8.33/6.25/53.33%. These are finite-time proof-of-recurrence statistics, not asymptotic survival estimates. Parent clusters only 2/4/5; the width-3 sign pattern of donor connected-rate difference is 2 positive, 1 negative, 2 tied.

**Strict boundary:** No evidence of learning, reproduction, SELF, goals or a separate autonomous temporal 'code'. The original persistence-class association in Experiment 108 remains conditional and valid at its defined horizon, but repeated surrogate patch-to-original-source similarity is affected by structural source-proximity, edit-location changes and nonconvergence. Results are not generalizable to the 130-parent catalogue without addressing the extreme null eligibility loss and intervention support shift. Every negative result is retained. No physical rule changes.

Further experiment, if warranted: separate the mechanism of raw-source coupling from organizational persistence by measuring the observable effect of intervention *in its own state coordinates* and carefully interpreting the exact counterfactual and positivity, rather than treating higher similarity to a state copied into the intervention as high-level autonomy.
